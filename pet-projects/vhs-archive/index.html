COMPLETE MAGNETIC CATALOGUE — 1958–1989

Twelve Archival Tapes

DIRECTOR BIOGRAPHY & PRESERVATION

Leon Vasari (1933–1994)

Working between northern port towns and remote frontier valleys from 1958 to 1989, Leon Vasari built twelve austere chamber features marked by wind-worn landscapes, magnetic silence, and slow geometric camera movement.

Following the closure of his Tokyo and Milan cutting rooms, every surviving 35mm workprint was struck onto heavy-stock archival VHS editions packaged in hand-screened cobalt and vermilion sleeves. This digital shelf preserves the twelve canonical releases in chronological sequence.
